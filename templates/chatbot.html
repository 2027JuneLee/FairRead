How to use the News Classifier
  1. Paste text directly into the input, then click the send icon.
  2. Paste a link (URL) to a news article. We’ll fetch the content and analyze it.
  3. Upload a PDF (or DOCX). We’ll extract text and analyze it.

What you’ll see on the right:

  • Bias Classification with Left/Center/Right scores
  • Reason explaining key signals
  • Summary of the article
  • Keywords detected

Tips

  • For paywalled pages, paste the text or upload a PDF.
  • Very long documents may take longer to process.
  • If extraction fails, try another link or paste the text.
