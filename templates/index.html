About FairRead

Welcome to FairRead! FairRead is a minimalistic and simple platform where users can instantly copy and paste any news articles or submit them in PDF format, and get a quick overview of its political bias, analysis, and overall summary of the article using AI. Whether you are a political junkie, researcher, or student, FairRead can detect bias in articles of any length and topic. Additionally, FairRead stores your articles in a log, so you can access them later and analyze your political bias better.

Features

Bias Verification

AI-powered news bias detection (left, center, right).

News Summary & Analysis

Reasoning for the bias and a summary of the article will aid your understanding of the news article.

User-based Analytics

Users can view their article history and their cumulative media bias leanings.
